Terror Byte

Created for a club game jam at SAU, the theme was "virus". With a virus eating away at your files you have to kill the viruses to get more files that give diffent buffs such as speed, damage or just more health, but becareful to not pick up corrupted files. you race against the clock to get to the end and fight the boss and rid yourself of the all the viruses.

Terror Byte was made with a team of four developers including me. With me working on the enemy AI.
