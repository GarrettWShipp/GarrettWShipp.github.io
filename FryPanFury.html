Fry Pan Fury

Created for my fundamental of game programming II class. I am the sole developer for this game. When I was developing this game I got inspiration from Slay the Spire, but I put a my own goofy twist on it. In which, Fry Pan Fury you play a barbarian that has convinced himself that his a wizard. You and your sturdy fry pan fight your way through the dungeon defeating enemies along the way.
