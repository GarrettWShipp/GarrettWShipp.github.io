Crown of Screws

Created for the 2024 LA Tech Game Jam, the theme was "Unwanted Abilities". Crash landed on a strange planet of machines, do your best to survive the waves as they surge forth.

In the Game Jam we won the awards “Best Game Overall” and “Developers' Choice".

Crown of Screws was developed by a team of six including me in which, I developed the enemy's logic using a state machine.
